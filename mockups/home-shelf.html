A toy shelf for the home screen

Play stays the one big button. Everything else she can do on her own sits on a wooden shelf underneath, and each activity is an object rather than a pill. Tap a toy to see how it answers.

The shelf is the app's button, made literal

Every control in Rooky already stands on a darker block and sinks onto it. Here the block is a real plank and the control is a real object. A toy squashes and hops when tapped, so the whole shelf says "press me" without a word.

The object is the shape

Two periwinkle pills are the same button to her. A jar and a chessboard never are. Each toy is a small picture of what it opens, like the sticker backgrounds, so its outline carries its identity and no new action colour is needed.

Colour stays where it already means something

Only Play is jade. The jar's lid is periwinkle because Mix always was. The shelf reuses the board's walnut, so it reads as part of the same table the board sits on. There is no gold anywhere on it, because gold means a reward.

Toys are not rewards

Trophies and stickers stay on their own tabs. The shelf holds only things that open a board, the one kind of activity that can never be a tab. That keeps it from turning into a second Things screen an inch above the Things tab.

It grows a plank at a time

Three toys fit on a plank. A fourth adds a second plank below and the wall scrolls while Play stays put. Adding an activity means drawing one object, not redesigning home.

Endless stays on the path

Endless belongs to a world, so it stays on the world's ribbon. The shelf holds the activities that are not about any one world.
