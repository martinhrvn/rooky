# Privacy Policy for Rooky

**Last updated: 14 August 2026**

Rooky is a chess-teaching app for young children, published by Martin Harvan
("we", "us") for Android under the package name `eu.hrvn.rooky`.

This policy explains what Rooky does with information. The short version is
that it does nothing with it: Rooky has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics
and no network connection of any kind, and nothing you or your child enters
ever leaves the device.

## Information we collect

**None.** Rooky collects no personal information, sends no data to us or to
anyone else, and contains no code that makes a network request. It does not ask
for any device permissions — no camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos
or storage access.

We have no server. There is nowhere for your data to go, and we could not
retrieve it if we wanted to.

## Information stored on your device

So that a child can pick up where they left off, Rooky saves the following in
the app's own private storage on the device:

- the **player name** and chosen **avatar** for each profile — the name is
  whatever is typed in when a profile is created, and can be anything you like;
- **game progress** — which levels have been finished, stars earned, fewest
  moves, and experience points;
- **rewards** — stickers collected and achievements unlocked;
- **settings** — the difficulty ceiling set for each profile, and which profile
  was last used.

This information stays on the device. It is not transmitted, backed up to us,
shared, sold, or used for advertising or profiling. Other apps on the device
cannot read it.

## Deleting the information

- **Inside the app** (Settings, behind the gear icon on the home screen):
  *Start over* clears the current player's progress and rewards, and
  *Remove player* deletes a profile entirely.
- **On the device**: uninstalling Rooky, or clearing its storage in Android
  Settings → Apps → Rooky → Storage, permanently removes everything the app has
  saved.

Because we never receive any of this data, there is nothing for us to delete on
our side, and no request needs to be made to us.

## Children's privacy

Rooky is designed for children, including children under 13. It is built to the
same principle throughout: it collects no personal information from anyone, of
any age.

Rooky contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no third-party analytics or
tracking SDKs, no social features, no chat, no user-generated content, and no
links out to the web or to other apps. There is nothing in it that lets a child
contact a stranger or spend money.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, or from adults,
because we do not collect personal information at all.

## Third parties

Rooky includes no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking services.

If you installed Rooky from Google Play, Google may collect information about
the installation and may gather crash and performance diagnostics as part of
the Android platform and the Play Store, independently of this app. That is
governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
by your own device settings, not by us — and any such report reaches us, if at
all, only as anonymous aggregate statistics in the Play Console.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address
and the date at the top will change. Since Rooky collects nothing, a change
would mean the app itself had changed, and that would arrive as an app update
you choose to install.
